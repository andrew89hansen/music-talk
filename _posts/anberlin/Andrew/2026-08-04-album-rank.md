# Anberlin album rank

This post will contain my rankings of the **seven** full length albums put out by Anberlin (pre Matty Mullins era).

That era of Anberlin was probably the most I ever listened to them and the band was a staple in my music library. Recently, they aren't a heavy hitter in the rotation anymore, they are one of my favorite bands of all time. 

They were introduced to me through a friend with whom I worked with at Acme markets back when I was only 17. It's wild to think I've been listening to this band for that long. 

I'll start from my least favorite and work my way up to the my most favorite. Below is more detail as to why I gave those rankings, if you care to read on. Now, I will say that I've been listening to these albums a lot lately and I can't promise it won't change in the future. For now, this where I stand. 

7. Blueprints for the Black Market (2003)
6. Lowborn (2014) 
5. Dark Is the Way, Light Is a Place (2010)
4. New Surrender (2008) 
3. Vital (2012) 
2. Never Take Friendship Personal (2005)
1. Cities (2007)

--- 

## Blueprints for the Black Market

The very first album. Although, I was first introduced to Anberlin with _Never Take Friendship Personal_.

This album sets up a lot of the foundation that I love about Anberlin. The fact that "Ready Fuels" is the first song should _probably_ bump this album up higher. It's one of their best. Also, "Undeveloped Story" is an absolute banger off this album. 

## Lowborn

I think _Lowborn_ fell short a bit. The fact that they announced they were breaking up in January 2014, six months before they broke up, may have hurt the album and my opinion of it. Knowing that it was going to be the last album, the bar was high.

I think "Hearing Voices", "Losing it All", "We Are Destroyer", and "Atonement" showcased some of their range. I connected with "Atonement" on a different level. Maybe it was because I knew, at the time, it was the end of the band I've come to love, or just the somber tone and lyrics that flow throughout.

> I've loved where I've been, Yes I love where I've been, But my heart's where I'm going

A simple line that resonates.

## Dark Is the Way, Light Is a Place

This album is almost in my top 3. This album almost feels like an extension of _Cities_. To me, it has a similar, yet more mature vibe than _Cities_.

I come back to "Art of War", "Depraved", and "Impossible" the most on the album, but the rest is quite solid.

## New Surrender

This was a tough one. There are **bangers** on this album. Starting right out of the gate with "The Resistance". I remember the crowd always getting a little when this song would come on. "Breaking" and "Blame Me! Blame Me!" are also great. 

Now, I think that this may be their best album in terms of showcasing their musical talents, if that makes sense. From the drums, base, guitar, lyrics, etc, it's all very tight and well put together. For some reason I don't find myself coming back to this one as much as the rest.

## Vital

This one is a sneaky one to me. After re-listening to every album, this one surprised me the most. Before listening again, I for sure thought this one would be closer to the bottom. Overall, it's a solid album. It has a strong start with "Self Starter" and "Little Tyrants". Even "Other Side" is a great song.

"Modern Age" may be in my top 5 Anberlin songs too. This album may be the one that I come back more as I get older.

## Never Take Friendship Personal

My personal introduction to the band. The title track may be the most known Anberlin song and the one that **always** killed live. Then, there's "Paperthin Hymn", "A Day Late", and so many other classics.

This album will always hold a special place to me because I watched them tour this album and it was on repeat for years.

> When memories fade, we've got each other.

## Cities

Ahhh, _Cities_. This album was on repeat for so many years. This was the album I used to introduce friends to Anberlin. The intro starts off with a punch with "Godspeed" and just continues to build. I think "A Whisper & A Clamor" _may_ be Anberlin's best song. It may be.

There are just so many great songs on this album. If I had to pick my weakest song...it would have to be "Reclusion". The closing of the album is the best they have; "Dismantle. Repair." followed "(*Fin)".

So many nights driving around blasting this album just screaming along with the lyrics.

> Hands, like secrets
> Are the hardest thing to keep from you
> Lines and phrases, like knives
> Your words can cut me through
